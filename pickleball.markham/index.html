How It Works

Pick the day you are free, look for a green Free block, and go during that window. If courts are full, use the Next Up board and rotate in.

Paid blocks are for Markham Pickleball Club (MPC) members/programs. Click here to learn more about MPC.

When You Arrive
  • Find the courts at Cornell Community Park.
  • If courts are full and new players arrive, use the Next Up rotation board.
  • Play doubles when people are waiting.
Bring
  • Paddle
  • Outdoor balls
  • Water
  • Shirt and non-marking court shoes
Rules
  • Games are to 11, or 20 minutes max.
  • Use the Next Up board when courts are full.
  • Do not enter nearby courts while play is active.
  • Clear the court when public or club time changes.
Next Up Board
  • Use the board when all courts are full and a new person or group arrives.
  • Put your paddle in the next open spot on the board.
  • Wait for the next finished game or 20-minute rotation.
  • When your spot is called, enter the assigned court and start the next game.
